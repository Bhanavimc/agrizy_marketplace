♻️ {{ t.composting_guide }}

Vermicomposting

  • • Use red wiggler earthworms
  • • Layer: dry leaves + kitchen waste + cow dung
  • • Keep moist, avoid direct sunlight
  • • Ready in 45-60 days

NADEP Composting

  • • Build brick tank (10x5x3 ft)
  • • Layer farm waste + cow dung + soil
  • • Plaster with mud to seal
  • • Ready in 90-120 days

🐛 {{ t.natural_pest_control }}

🍆 Neem Spray

Crush neem leaves, soak in water overnight, strain and spray. Controls aphids, whiteflies, caterpillars.

🌶 Garlic-Chili Spray

Blend garlic + green chilies + water. Strain and spray. Repels most sucking insects.

🐝 Pheromone Traps

Place in fields to attract and trap male moths. Reduces pest population naturally.

🌱 {{ t.organic_fertilizers }}

Jeevamrutha

10kg cow dung + 10L cow urine + 2kg jaggery + 2kg pulse flour + handful of soil in 200L water. Ferment 48 hrs.

Panchagavya

Cow dung, urine, milk, curd, ghee. Mix and ferment for 15 days. 3% solution as foliar spray.

Fish Amino Acid

Fish waste + jaggery (1:1). Ferment 15-20 days. Rich in nitrogen and growth hormones.

Green Manure

Grow dhaincha, sunhemp, or cowpea and plough back into soil before flowering. Adds nitrogen.
